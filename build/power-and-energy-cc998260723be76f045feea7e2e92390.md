# Power and Energy

Power and energy engineering studies how electrical energy is generated, transformed, delivered, and used efficiently. It connects generation, conversion, transmission, and end-use systems.

## Core topics

### Generation

Electrical power is generated from sources such as:

- fossil and gas plants
- hydroelectric systems
- wind and solar generation
- nuclear power
- distributed generation

### Transmission and distribution

Power systems move electricity from generation sites to users through transmission networks, substations, transformers, and distribution lines.

### Conversion and control

Power electronics converts electrical energy between forms and conditions, supporting:

- motor drives
- battery systems
- converters and inverters
- renewable integration

### Efficiency and reliability

Power engineering is especially concerned with:

- losses and heat management
- grid stability
- protection and fault response
- voltage regulation
- sustainability and resilience

## Why it matters

Power systems are central to transportation, industry, buildings, and infrastructure. The ability to generate, distribute, and manage energy reliably shapes economic productivity and societal stability.

## Summary

Power and energy engineering addresses one of the most important practical goals of electrical engineering: delivering reliable, efficient, and sustainable electrical energy at scale.
