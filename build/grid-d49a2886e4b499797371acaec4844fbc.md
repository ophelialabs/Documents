## The Golden Dome 
Is a multi-layered American Defense shield (consisting of **ground batteries** and thousands of **satellites**) designed to protect the U.S. from advanced ballistic, hypersonic, and cruise missille attacks (is it though? e.g., Apt/Aladin?).
- **The Grid Threat**: A primary objective of the Golden Dome is to protect the U.S. electrical grid from an EMP. Just a few high-altitude nuclear detonations or EMP weapons could "theoretically" fry electrical infrastructure and knock out power nationwide. 
- **The Quantum Bubble**: Traditional radar and communications can be jammed or spoofed by electronic warfare (see: Rohde & Schwarz). To combat this, the Pentagon is funding quantum sensing and quantum computing programs. These technologies rely on the fundamental properties of particles-like "quantum bubbles" or localized quantum states-to create highly resilient, unhackable communication networks and ultra-precise navigation. This creates a protective "bubble" of data integrity around the denfensive shield, ensuring satellites can coordinate even in the "fog of war".

## Sigfox / 0g
Serves as the **unhackable, low-power nervous system** that keeps the electrical grid alive when the Golden Dome is ***actively*** defending against an attack.
