The Mechanical Balance of Plant (BOP) and environmental containment control systems form the critical backbone of nuclear plant safety, equipment protection, and regulatory compliance. A Senior Systems Engineer – HVAC / Nuclear serves as the primary technical authority for specialized thermal-fluid systems, ensuring that both normal operations and emergency mitigation systems perform flawlessly under strict nuclear quality standards.
This role requires a unique intersection of advanced mechanical design, thermal-fluid analytics, nuclear quality assurance, and practical plant operations knowledge.

## Specialized System Domain
Include Light Water, Liquid Metal, and High-Temperature Gas Reactors

In a Boiling Water Reactor (BWR) environment, HVAC and cooling systems are not just for comfort; they are critical safety and investment-protection barriers:

* Containment & Primary Structures Cooling: Managing drywell cooling, suppression pool atmosphere, and containment isolation systems to handle severe thermal loads and pressure profiles.
* Safety-Related HVAC (Habitability): Designing Main Control Room (MCR) habitability systems, emergency diesel generator building ventilation, and engineered safety features (ESF) atmospheric cleanup systems.
* Plant Chilled Water & Heat Sinks: Engineering the vital chilled water loops that service safety-related air handling units and heat exchangers.
* Radiological Ventilation Control: Designing pressure cascades (maintaining negative pressure in contaminated zones) and high-efficiency particulate air (HEPA) / charcoal filtration configurations to prevent the release of airborne radionuclides.

## Core Engineering & Analytical Activities

* Design & Flow Diagrams: Develop, review, and finalize highly technical D&IDs (Duct & Instrumentation Diagrams) and P&IDs (Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams) tracking complex fluid and airflow paths.
* Thermal-Fluid Calculations: Own and validate rigorous analytical design models, including:
* Heat Load Calculations: Determining worst-case bounding ambient, process, and radiological heat generation rates under normal, upset, and accident conditions.
   * Pressure Drop Calculations: Running hydraulic and aerodynamic piping/ducting loop analyses to size fans, pumps, dampers, and valves.
   * Equipment Selection: Specifying nuclear-grade chillers, air handling units, dampers, and filtration trains.
* Quality Assurance & Compliance: Execute all design work strictly within the boundaries of NQA-1 (Nuclear Quality Assurance-1) and ISO 9000 frameworks. This requires meticulous configuration control, safety-critical verification, independent design reviews, and commercial grade dedication (CGD) processing.
* Operational Integration: Leverage operational insights—often backed by a Senior Reactor Operator (SRO) license qualification or equivalent training—to ensure system designs seamlessly align with plant operating procedures, tech specs, emergency operating procedures (EOPs), and human-system interfaces.
