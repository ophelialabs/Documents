# Electronics

Electronics focuses on the design and use of semiconductor devices to process signals and control electrical energy. It bridges device physics and practical circuit design.

## Core topics

### Semiconductor devices

- diodes
- bipolar junction transistors
- field-effect transistors
- operational amplifiers
- MOSFETs

These devices enable switching, amplification, and signal conditioning.

### Analog electronics

Analog electronics deals with continuous signals and includes:

- amplifiers
- filters
- oscillators
- rectifiers
- comparators

### Digital electronics

Digital electronics uses discrete logic levels for computation and control. This includes:

- logic gates
- flip-flops and memory
- digital signal processing building blocks
- microcontrollers and embedded logic

### Design trade-offs

Electronics design often involves balancing:

- speed and power
- gain and noise
- efficiency and thermal limits
- cost and complexity

## Why it matters

Electronics is essential to nearly every modern system: sensors, computers, radio hardware, power converters, automation platforms, and consumer devices.

## Summary

Electronics is the practical embodiment of circuit theory and device physics. It turns basic electrical behavior into useful systems that compute, measure, amplify, and control.
