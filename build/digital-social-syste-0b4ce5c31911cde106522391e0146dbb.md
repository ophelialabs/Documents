# Digital Social Systems

Digital social systems study how online communities, platforms, and algorithmic infrastructures shape behavior, identity, and collective action.

## Why it matters

Digital environments are now central to social interaction, commerce, governance, and identity formation. Understanding them is essential to analyzing modern society.

## Key topics

- online communities
- identity performance and self-presentation
- networked influence
- social comparison
- coordinated action
- platform-mediated norms

## System dynamics

Digital systems influence behavior through:

- recommendation algorithms
- engagement incentives
- social feedback loops
- visibility and ranking
- participatory culture

## Social consequences

- polarization
- attention capture
- identity fragmentation
- misinformation amplification
- civic mobilization and activism

## Summary

Digital social systems show how communication technology reshapes social organization. They connect groups, institutions, media systems, and individual identity in an increasingly algorithmic environment.
