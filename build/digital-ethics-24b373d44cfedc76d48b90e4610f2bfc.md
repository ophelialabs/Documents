# Digital Ethics

Digital ethics concerns the moral and social implications of technology, information systems, and platform design. It explores how digital systems influence fairness, autonomy, privacy, trust, and social responsibility.

## Why it matters

As digital systems become central to everyday life, ethical questions become unavoidable. These concerns affect business, governance, public life, and individual agency.

## Core themes

- privacy and consent
- fairness and bias
- transparency and accountability
- manipulation and persuasion
- autonomy and agency
- social harm and externalities

## Key questions

- Who benefits from a digital system?
- Who bears the costs?
- Are decisions transparent and explainable?
- Does the system empower or manipulate users?
- How should harms be governed and prevented?

## Applications

- algorithmic design
- synthetic media governance
- platform moderation
- data ethics
- AI policy and safety

## Summary

Digital ethics is the normative foundation for governance, media trust, and public responsibility in technologically mediated life.
