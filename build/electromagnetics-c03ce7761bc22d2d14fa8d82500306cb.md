# Electromagnetics

Electromagnetics studies the interaction between electric and magnetic fields and how these fields propagate energy and information through space. It is one of the most foundational areas of electrical engineering.

## Core topics

### Electric fields

Electric fields arise from charge distributions and govern electrostatics, capacitance, and energy storage.

### Magnetic fields

Magnetic fields arise from current flow and magnetic materials and govern inductance, motors, and transformers.

### Maxwell's equations

Maxwell's equations unify electric and magnetic phenomena and describe how time-varying fields generate each other.

### Wave propagation

Electromagnetics explains how waves travel through free space and materials. This is essential for:

- antennas
- radio systems
- microwave engineering
- optics and photonics interfaces

### Transmission lines and antennas

These engineering structures are used to guide and radiate electromagnetic energy.

## Why it matters

Electromagnetics underpins communications, power transfer, sensing, and high-frequency electronics. It explains how signals move through wires, waveguides, and the air.

## Study themes

- electrostatics
- magnetostatics
- Faraday's law
- Ampère's law
- wave propagation
- boundary conditions and materials

## Summary

Electromagnetics is the physical basis of many electrical systems. It connects the abstract equations of physics with real systems for communication, energy transfer, and sensing.
