# Fuel Cycle and Materials

The nuclear fuel cycle spans the full life of nuclear materials: mining, conversion, enrichment, fabrication, use in the reactor, storage, and disposition of spent fuel or recycled material. Materials performance is central to every stage.

## Why it matters

Fuel cycle decisions affect economics, waste, proliferation risk, and long-term stewardship. Materials must survive high temperatures, intense radiation fields, corrosion, and mechanical stress.

## Major stages

### Front end

- uranium mining and milling
- conversion to uranium hexafluoride
- enrichment
- fuel fabrication

### In-reactor operation

- fuel cladding and structural integrity
- fission product buildup
- burnup and performance limits
- fuel swelling and embrittlement

### Back end

- spent fuel storage
- reprocessing or direct disposal
- high-level waste management
- repository planning and environmental protection

## Materials concerns

- zirconium alloy cladding performance
- corrosion and stress-corrosion cracking
- irradiation embrittlement
- thermal creep
- waste form stability
