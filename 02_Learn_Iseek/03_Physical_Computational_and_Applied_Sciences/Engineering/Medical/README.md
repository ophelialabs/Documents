#### Surgical and Psychological Barriers
- **Physical Drape Separators**: In clinical settings, a physical sterile drape or "curtain" is placed between the surgical/injection field and the patient’s field of view.
- **Psychological Shielding**: During awake procedures (like deep brain mapping or localized cranial injections), this curtain hides the complex optical cameras, robotic arms, and medical instruments from the patient. This maintains a calm state, preventing spikes in blood pressure or stress that could skew neural mapping data.
