# Communications

Communications engineering studies how information is transmitted, received, and recovered reliably across channels. It combines signal processing, electromagnetics, modulation, and network concepts.

## Core concepts

### Modulation

Signals are modified to carry information over a medium. Common methods include:

- amplitude modulation
- frequency modulation
- phase modulation
- digital modulation schemes

### Channel effects

Communication systems must cope with:

- noise
- attenuation
- interference
- distortion
- bandwidth constraints

### Detection and error correction

Receivers recover information using filtering, demodulation, synchronization, and coding techniques.

## Why it matters

Communications is central to wireless networks, internet infrastructure, broadcasting, satellite systems, and embedded sensor networks.

## Summary

Communications engineering focuses on reliable transfer of information through physical media and electromagnetic channels. It is deeply connected to signals, electromagnetics, and digital systems.
